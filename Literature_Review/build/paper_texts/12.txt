                                            of individual neurons and local field potentials. Optogenetics has revolutionized
                                            neuroscience studies by offering selective and fast control of targeted neurons and
                                            neuron populations. The combination of these two techniques is crucial for causal
                                            investigation of neural circuits and understanding their functional connectivity. However,
                                            electrical artifacts generated by light stimulation interfere with neural recordings and
